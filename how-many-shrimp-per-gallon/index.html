Stocking guide

How many shrimp per gallon?

Two different questions hide in this one: how many to buy, and how many the tank can eventually hold. The first is the one that matters, because a healthy colony sets its own ceiling.

By the shrimp desk at Pet Zone Convoy, San Diego · Updated October 8, 2026

The short answer

In one line

Start with ten to fifteen dwarf shrimp in a tank of five gallons or more. Let them breed up. A mature, well-planted tank will comfortably hold several shrimp per gallon, and the colony levels off on its own based on food and surface area.

How many to start with

5 gallons10 Neocaridina. A workable minimum size; changes in the water happen faster, so keep it stable.
10 gallons10–15 Neocaridina, or 10–15 Caridina in a tank already stable for months. The easiest size for a first colony.
20 gallons and up15–25 to start. Room for a large colony, plus Amano shrimp for algae.

Why ten or more even in a small tank? Shrimp spend a lot of time hiding, especially at first, and with five you may see none of them. A group of ten also gives a good chance of both sexes and enough breeding pairs to establish. Buying five tends to produce a colony that never quite takes off.

How many a tank can hold

Dwarf shrimp produce very little waste, so the limit is rarely water quality. It is food and grazing surface. A densely planted, mature tank with moss, leaf litter and hardscape can support far more shrimp than a bare tank of the same size, and a colony will grow into that capacity over months. A commonly quoted ceiling for Neocaridina is up to around ten per gallon in a well-run tank.

You do not need to manage this closely. When a colony hits its ceiling, breeding slows. If you want more shrimp, add surface area — more moss, more leaves, more hardscape — rather than more food.

Amano shrimp for algae

About one Amano per two to three gallons for algae control; six in a 20-gallon planted tank is a good start. They need a tank of ten gallons or more. See the Amano shrimp guide.

With fish in the tank

Adult numbers stay roughly the same, but expect few babies to survive unless the tank has dense cover. Add shrimp before fish if you can. The tank mates guide covers which fish are safe.

Questions we get asked

How many shrimp should I put in a 5 gallon tank?

Start with about ten Neocaridina. Fewer than that and you will rarely see them, and the colony may struggle to establish. They will breed up to fit the tank.

How many cherry shrimp in a 10 gallon tank?

Ten to fifteen to start. A mature, planted 10-gallon tank can hold a colony of many dozens once they breed.

Can you overstock a shrimp tank?

It is hard to overstock with dwarf shrimp because their waste output is very low. The real limits are food and grazing surface, and a colony slows its own breeding when it reaches them.

How many Amano shrimp do I need?

About one per two to three gallons for algae control, in a tank of ten gallons or more.

Neocaridina davidi

Snowball shrimp

Opaque milky white, and named for the eggs females carry, which look like tiny snowballs. As easy as a cherry, and one of the most striking shrimp you can put over dark substrate.

By the shrimp desk at Pet Zone Convoy, San Diego · Updated October 8, 2026

What they are

Snowball shrimp are a white color morph of Neocaridina davidi, the same species as red cherry, blue dream and every other Neocaridina color. That means the same easy care — and it means they will crossbreed with other Neocaridina. The name comes from the eggs: where most Neocaridina carry yellow or green eggs, snowball females carry white ones, clustered under the tail like a small snowball.

Color varies from translucent white to solid opaque white between individuals and lines. Over black soil and green moss, even the paler ones stand out.

Water and setup

GH 6–8 (tolerating to about 12), KH 2–8, pH 6.5–8.0, TDS 150–250, 65–78°F. In San Diego, treated tap water works; it runs harder than this range, so cutting it roughly half and half with RO is optional. Chloramine must be treated every time. See San Diego tap water for shrimp.

A five-gallon tank will hold a colony and ten is easier. Use a sponge filter, plenty of moss for shrimplets, and dark substrate — a white shrimp on white sand disappears.

Keeping the line white

  • One color per tank. Mixed with reds or blues, the offspring drift toward brown wild-type within a few generations.
  • Remove off-color shrimp that show patches of brown or clear if you want the colony to stay white.
  • Steady, low-stress conditions. Stress and unstable water wash out color in any Neocaridina.

Questions we get asked

Why are they called snowball shrimp?

Because the females carry white eggs that cluster under the tail like a small snowball. Most other Neocaridina carry yellow or green eggs.

Are snowball shrimp easy to keep?

Yes. They are Neocaridina, with the same easy care as cherry shrimp, and in San Diego they can live on treated tap water.

Can snowball shrimp live with cherry shrimp?

They can share a tank safely, but they are the same species and will crossbreed. The offspring drift toward brown wild-type, so keep one color per tank if you want to keep the white.

What substrate is best for snowball shrimp?

Dark substrate such as black soil or dark gravel. It makes the white stand out and helps the shrimp feel secure.
